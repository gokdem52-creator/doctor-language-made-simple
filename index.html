    Understanding, not just words

    Literal translation of jargon is still jargon. Simplifying first keeps meaning intact.

    Safer care

    Patients who understand diagnoses and instructions follow treatment plans more reliably.

    Built for everyone

    Helps non-native speakers and anyone overwhelmed by clinical language.
